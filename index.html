Good to know

Quick answers about booking, packages and visiting.

How do I book the La Lola booth?

Choose your event type and send the inquiry form above, or email bistronomia.sales@bistro.com.ph. You can also message us on Instagram.

What’s included in a Carrito package?

Booth setup and La Lola cones. Every cone comes with 4 classic churros and a chocolate dip. Packages range from 50 to 100 cones.

Is there an extra fee for the venue?

Yes. A mobilization fee is added based on your event location. Our team will confirm it when you inquire.

Can I add Munch Stix to my event?

Yes. Munch Stix are available as add-ons in sets of 10 to 50 pieces.

Where can I get La Lola churros?

Visit any of our four branches listed above, or order delivery through Bistro Delivers. Opening hours can vary by mall, so check Instagram or the map listing before you go.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Order La Lola delivery through Bistro Delivers using the Order button on this page.

Where can I see the full menu?

Use the Download menu (PDF) button in the Order section to see the full La Lola menu.

Are seasonal items always available?

Seasonal specials like Boo-reo Churros come and go, so ask your branch about availability. Follow us on Instagram to see what’s new.

What about allergens and ingredients?

If you or your guests have allergies, please ask your branch or email us before ordering so we can give you accurate information.

Where can I see the latest deals?

Follow @lalolachurreria_ph on Instagram. Exclusive bundles for bistroBFF members are announced through bistroBFF.
